# Opportunity Vault

The Opportunity Vault captures raw observations before they are promoted to product candidates. It is intentionally faster and less committal than product intake.

## Integrity rules

- Record what was observed separately from the initial hypothesis.
- Confidence describes the evidence record, not the attractiveness of the opportunity.
- `Things That Shouldn't Be True` marks abnormalities such as disproportionate pricing, avoidable frustration, waste, weak incumbent products, or surprising customer behavior.
- A conversion creates a linked product and preserves the original vault record.
- Conversion is not validation and does not authorize spending, supplier contact, or publishing.

## Statuses

Inbox, Needs context, Worth investigating, Converted to product candidate, Rejected, and Archived.

The rapid-entry form requires only a title, observation, source, observation date, confidence, and submitter. Optional context can be added when known without fabricating missing detail.
