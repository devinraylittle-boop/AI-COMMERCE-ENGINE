## Method quality

- Where did the interviewer lead the participant?
- Which question was confusing?
- Which answer described hypothetical intent rather than behavior?
- What important follow-up was missed?
- Did the participant appear to give a socially desirable answer?
- What alternate explanation fits the same facts?
- Did the interview enter unnecessary sensitive territory?
- Can another reviewer trace every conclusion to the notes?
