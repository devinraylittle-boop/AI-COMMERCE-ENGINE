## Required first inquiry

Send the same questions to every candidate so comparison remains fair:

1. Are you the brand owner, manufacturer, or authorized distributor for the exact SKU?
2. May Little Built LLC sell the product on its own US ecommerce storefront?
3. Wholesale unit price by quantity tier and current MOQ?
4. Sample price and whether sample cost is credited to a later order?
5. Current inventory, backorder policy, production lead time, and ship-from location?
6. Unit and master-carton dimensions and weights?
7. Materials, load limits, care instructions, warranty, and test documentation?
8. Defect allowance, replacement process, return restrictions, and damage responsibility?
9. MAP, channel, photography, trademark, and product-copy restrictions?
10. Dropship availability, per-order fee, handling time, blind-shipping capability, and inventory feed?
11. Payment terms and all fees not included in the unit price?
12. Can they provide a W-9 for a US entity or full foreign legal/export identity and factory details?

## Automatic rejection

Reject or pause a path when it:

- cannot establish legal identity or authorization;
- refuses exact unit/carton dimensions or material information;
- claims universal fit or unsupported loads;
- requires inventory beyond the $250 company ceiling;
- prevents honest product comparisons or disclosure of limitations;
- produces landed cost above the Founding Five gate;
- requires exclusivity, long commitments, or nonrefundable bulk payment before samples;
- will not describe defect and replacement handling;
- uses copied brand assets, ambiguous trademarks, or unverifiable compliance claims.

## Recommended contact order

1. YouCopia wholesale
2. Joseph Joseph trade
3. Faire access for the named wholesale alternatives
4. DEKAVA only as an additional cabinet-organizer comparison

This order favors low-MOQ, branded, US-facing paths before factory-direct or private-label complexity. It may produce thinner margins, but Company 001 first needs a trustworthy product and a functioning store—not a container-load commitment.
