About Little Built

We are not here to reinvent the home.

We want to improve the small systems inside it—carefully, honestly, and without asking people to remodel their lives around another product.

Our current best understanding

Better systems.
Less frustration.

Little Built is the working brand for Company 001, the first real case study inside ACE. The first collection begins with reversible organization because it lets a customer improve an awkward space without drilling, construction, or a permanent commitment.

We are starting with three candidates, not a giant catalog. The goal is not to look complete. It is to make every decision legible: what the product solves, where it fails, what remains unknown, and whether a less expensive answer is enough.

If the products disappear and this idea still makes sense, we have a brand. If not, we only had a catalog.
