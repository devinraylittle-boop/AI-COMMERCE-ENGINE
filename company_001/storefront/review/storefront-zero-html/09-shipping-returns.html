Policy status / draft only

No orders means no promises we cannot keep.

Storefront Zero does not accept purchases. The principles below are requirements for a future policy, not current commercial terms.

Shipping standard

Know the full cost before payment.

  • Delivery estimate and shipping charge shown before checkout
  • Tracking provided when available from the selected service
  • No “ships today” claim without verified handling capacity
  • Damage and missing-package process written in plain language

Return standard

Prevent the return before the sale.

  • Complete fit guidance on every product page
  • No universal-fit or unsupported performance claims
  • A clear return window before checkout
  • Defect, damage, and preference returns treated distinctly
Current statusDraft principles only

Final terms require a selected product, fulfillment path, real shipping cost, and legal review appropriate to the launch market.
