# Company 001 — The Founding Five v0.1

**Decision date:** 2026-07-14

**Mission:** Build a small, profitable ecommerce business that teaches ACE something every week.

## Decision

Build the storefront around one clear promise:

> Small organization tools that fit real homes, require no permanent installation, and remove daily friction without creating more clutter.

Advance three products to sourcing and sample verification:

1. Tool-Free Adjustable Under-Sink Caddy
2. Pull-Out Pantry Caddy
3. Expandable Drawer Organizer

Keep two reserves:

4. No-Drill Shower Caddy
5. Whole-Bag Airtight Pantry Starter Set

These are product specifications, not selected suppliers or authorized purchases. Existing branded products are evidence and quality benchmarks; ACE has no right to resell, copy, or imply affiliation with those brands.

## Why these five

All five are:

- expected to retail below $75;
- understandable in one sentence;
- non-electronic;
- usable without apparel-style sizing;
- capable of honest pros, cons, and no-buy guidance;
- represented by multiple existing product approaches;
- compatible with a focused organization storefront;
- capable of bundling without requiring a large catalog.

The launch trio avoids the reserves' largest risks: adhesive/surface failures and food-contact documentation. The trio also shares a coherent customer job—make awkward kitchen and utility storage easier to see, reach, and reset.

## Launch economics gates

No candidate becomes a sellable SKU until a real supplier quote and sample support all of the following:

| Gate | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Retail price | $24–$49 for individual launch products; no product above $75 |
| Landed product cost | Target no more than 30% of retail; reject above 35% without demonstrated higher AOV or repeat value |
| Pre-ad contribution margin | At least 45% after landed cost, packaging, payment fees, and a conservative return allowance |
| Shipping | Standard parcel; no oversize surcharge; packaged weight and dimensional weight recorded from a real sample |
| Quality | Two samples from independent supplier paths pass the product-specific checks |
| Supply | At least two legitimate supplier paths or one exceptionally strong domestic path with a documented contingency |
| Claims | Every material, load, compatibility, and care claim supported by supplier documentation and sample verification |
| Returns | Clear pre-purchase fit screen and no unsupported universal-fit claim |

These are operating thresholds, not observed economics.

## Three-product storefront

The first catalog is intentionally small:

- **Reach:** Tool-Free Adjustable Under-Sink Caddy
- **Retrieve:** Pull-Out Pantry Caddy
- **Reset:** Expandable Drawer Organizer

Suggested bundle concept: **The Kitchen Friction Kit**. Bundle pricing waits for real landed costs; no discount is promised yet.

The storefront requires only:

1. Home
2. Shop
3. Three product pages
4. Our standard: how products earn a place
5. Shipping and returns
6. Contact
7. Privacy and terms

No blog engine, marketplace, AI assistant, loyalty program, quiz, or dashboard is required for launch.

## Immediate work

1. Identify two legitimate supplier paths for each launch candidate.
2. Obtain written unit, MOQ, packaging, shipping-origin, lead-time, defect, replacement, and material details.
3. Buy no inventory. Request approval for at most two samples per candidate after quote review.
4. Test the samples using the product-specific checks in the five briefs.
5. Select one supplier and final SKU per product only after economics and quality pass.
6. Then build the storefront with real photos, dimensions, claims, prices, and policies.

## Stop condition

If no launch candidate can meet the cost, quality, shipping, and claims gates, do not lower the standard to preserve the catalog. Replace the weakest candidate and keep the store at three products or fewer.
