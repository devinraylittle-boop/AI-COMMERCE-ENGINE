# Founding Product 01 — Tool-Free Adjustable Under-Sink Caddy

**Status:** advance to supplier quotes and samples; not approved for sale

## What problem does it solve?

Cleaning bottles and supplies become difficult to see and retrieve in cabinets interrupted by plumbing. A narrow, tool-free caddy groups frequently used items and can be removed as one unit.

## Who is it for?

Someone with a dry cabinet, adequate measured clearance, and supplies that fit the published load and dimensions.

## Who should not buy it?

Anyone with an active leak, mold, pest issue, unsafe chemical mix, insufficient clearance, or more inventory than the cabinet can reasonably hold.

## Why this one?

The desired specification is narrow, freestanding, washable, adjustable around bottle heights, and removable without drilling. A current benchmark publishes a no-install design and 15.1 × 8.0 × 12.3-inch dimensions ([YouCopia trade sheet](https://www.theinspiredhomeshow.com/wp-content/uploads/gravity_forms/9-ebc9f8f059e58b737f39a86d0cb6a220/2024/03/YouCopia-Collections-Free-Up-Valuable-Under-Sink-Space.pdf)).

## Why not a cheaper alternative?

A plain bin is better when the customer only needs containment. This product earns a premium only if its handle, dividers, base stability, and access materially reduce retrieval friction.

## Why not a more expensive alternative?

A mounted pull-out may offer smoother access and higher capacity, but installation and compatibility raise cost and return risk. IKEA's $34.99 mounted benchmark shows contradictory fit experiences around cabinets and disposals ([IKEA UTRUSTA](https://www.ikea.com/us/en/p/utrusta-under-sink-organiser-60612399/)).

## What evidence supports the recommendation?

Public product and editorial evidence repeatedly identifies plumbing clearance, access, decluttering, and fit as the decision variables. Consumer Reports also warns that bulky door storage can collide with plumbing or the basin ([Consumer Reports](https://www.consumerreports.org/home-garden/home-organization/how-to-organize-the-cabinet-under-a-sink-a1194960617/)).

## What remains unanswered?

Actual landed cost, packaged weight, supplier consistency, plastic quality, handle performance, base stability, cleaning, load limit, and return rate.

## Sample checks

- fits the declared minimum cabinet envelope without contacting pipes or hinges;
- remains stable at the declared load when fully pulled and carried;
- accepts common tall bottles without tipping;
- contains a small bottle leak without material damage;
- cleans and dries without trapping water or odor;
- survives 500 pull, lift, and reset cycles without cracking or wheel failure;
- matches every published measurement and claim.
