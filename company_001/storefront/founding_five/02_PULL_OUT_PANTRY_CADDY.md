# Founding Product 02 — Pull-Out Pantry Caddy

**Status:** advance to supplier quotes and samples; not approved for sale

## What problem does it solve?

Deep shelves hide smaller packages and force repeated reaching or unloading. A pull-out caddy moves one stable category from shelf to counter.

## Who is it for?

Someone with a measured shelf deep enough for the product and a recurring group of packages that is hard to see or retrieve.

## Who should not buy it?

Someone whose primary problem is excess inventory, active pests, spills that require cleanup, or a shelf too shallow or weak for the loaded caddy.

## Why this one?

The desired specification combines a washable high-walled tray, adjustable dividers, stable wheels or low-friction runners, and handles. A current benchmark is listed at $24.99 with two dividers and wheels ([YouCopia RollOut](https://youcopia.com/products/rollout-pantry-drawer-8)).

## Why not a cheaper alternative?

A basic clear bin is sufficient when visibility and lifting solve the problem. Wheels and dividers deserve a premium only when they improve retrieval without reducing usable capacity.

## Why not a more expensive alternative?

Installed pantry drawers can carry more weight but require hardware, cabinet compatibility, and greater commitment. Company 001 should first test the reversible version.

## What evidence supports the recommendation?

Retail offerings show current spending on movable pantry organization. The prior signal sprint also found that capacity, visibility, and maintenance must remain separate from pest and excess-inventory problems.

## What remains unanswered?

Landed cost, real wheel durability, pull force under load, shelf scratching, usable internal width, packaged weight, supplier redundancy, and whether customers will pay more than for a bin.

## Sample checks

- rolls straight and remains stable at the declared load;
- dividers do not pop free under repeated use;
- handles do not flex or crack when carried;
- base does not scratch a representative laminated shelf;
- survives 1,000 loaded pull-and-return cycles;
- cleans without water entrapment;
- dimensions match the listing within the declared tolerance.
