# Founding Product 05 — Whole-Bag Airtight Pantry Starter Set

**Status:** reserve; do not source until food-contact documentation and dimensional shipping economics are verified

## What problem does it solve?

Opened dry-food packaging spills, hides remaining quantity, and can be vulnerable to pantry pests. A right-capacity sealed container can hold an entire common package.

## Who is it for?

Someone with verified shelf clearance who regularly buys the declared package sizes and wants visible, washable dry-food containment.

## Who should not buy it?

Anyone with an active infestation, contaminated food, excess inventory, packages that do not fit, or a preference to keep food in original packaging. Containers do not treat pests.

## Why this one?

The desired specification begins with whole-package capacity, replaceable lids/seals, stackability, washability, and traceable food-contact documentation. OXO publishes capacity guidance and a six-piece benchmark around $56 ([OXO assortment](https://www.oxo.com/shop/kitchenware/pop-containers.html), [size guide](https://www.oxo.com/blog/cleaning-and-organizing/guide-oxo-pop-containers)).

## Why not a cheaper alternative?

Original packaging or inexpensive jars are better when they seal, fit, and remain visible. A set earns a premium only through verified seals, useful capacities, cleanability, and replacement parts.

## Why not a more expensive alternative?

Glass may offer different durability and material preferences but adds weight and breakage. Premium styling is not a sufficient reason to raise price.

## What evidence supports the recommendation?

University of Minnesota Extension recommends tightly closed glass, metal, or heavy plastic containers as one part of pantry-pest prevention while emphasizing cleanup, rotation, and disposal of contaminated food ([UMN Extension](https://extension.umn.edu/product-and-houseplant-pests/pantry-pests-insects-found-stored-food)). Existing retail sets have large review corpora, but also lid-durability complaints.

## What remains unanswered?

Food-contact compliance, seal performance, lid replacements, odor/staining, drop durability, dimensional shipping cost, supplier auditability, and whether a useful starter set can meet margin gates.

## Sample checks

- no quote advances without traceable food-contact documentation for the exact material and factory;
- capacities verified against declared common package weights/volumes;
- leak, seal-cycle, drop, dishwasher, stain, odor, and stacking tests;
- lids and seals must be replaceable or the limitation stated prominently;
- packaging and shipping test for cracks and dimensional-weight cost;
- no pest-treatment or universal-freshness claim.
