# Founding Product 03 — Expandable Drawer Organizer

**Status:** advance to supplier quotes and samples; not approved for sale

## What problem does it solve?

Mixed utensils become difficult to find and can jam a drawer. An expandable organizer creates stable zones while using the available width.

## Who is it for?

Someone whose drawer falls inside the published minimum and maximum dimensions and whose utensils fit the compartment depth and height.

## Who should not buy it?

Someone with a shallow, unusually narrow, obstructed, or overfilled drawer—or someone who can solve the issue by removing unused utensils.

## Why this one?

The desired specification is expandable, non-slip, washable, low-profile, and explicit about internal as well as external dimensions. Joseph Joseph's current benchmarks sell near $26–$32 and publish minimum drawer-height constraints ([expandable organizer](https://us.josephjoseph.com/products/drawerstore-expanding-cutlery-utensil-gadgets-organizer-stone), [tiered organizer](https://us.josephjoseph.com/products/drawerstore-expanding-tiered-cutlery-utensil-gadget-organizer-stone)).

## Why not a cheaper alternative?

Fixed trays are better for standard drawers when their dimensions already fit. Expandability earns a premium only if it stays locked, wastes little space, and avoids rattling.

## Why not a more expensive alternative?

Custom inserts can maximize every inch but are harder to ship, replace, and reuse after a move. A reversible organizer better fits Company 001's initial promise.

## What evidence supports the recommendation?

Multiple established brands offer adjustable or modular organizers, demonstrating a mature customer comparison set. The fit requirement is measurable before purchase, and the product avoids installation, electronics, and safety claims.

## What remains unanswered?

Landed cost, material odor, expansion-lock durability, non-slip performance, utensil fit across households, packaged dimensional weight, and return behavior.

## Sample checks

- fits every declared drawer dimension and leaves safe top clearance;
- expansion mechanism holds through 1,000 open/close cycles;
- does not slide in a representative finished drawer;
- no sharp edges, odor, warping, or inaccessible cleaning channels;
- common utensils do not bridge compartments and jam the drawer;
- listing measurements match the sample.
