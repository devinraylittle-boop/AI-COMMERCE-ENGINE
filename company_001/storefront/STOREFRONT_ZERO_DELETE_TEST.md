# Storefront Zero — Delete Test

**Date:** 2026-07-14  
**Working brand:** Little Built  
**Promise:** Small changes. Better everyday living.  
**Position:** Reversible organization for real homes.

## Test

Evaluate the brand and experience with all three candidate products, the Under-Sink Caddy removed, and both the Under-Sink and Pantry Caddies removed. The executable test operates on the same catalog model used by the site.

## Result

**Pass.** The promise, recommendation standard, About narrative, navigation, FAQ, and policy principles do not depend on any individual product. With one product remaining, the company still stands for a measurable behavior: make small, reversible improvements; diagnose before selling; establish fit before features; and choose truth over urgency.

## What the test does not prove

- That customers value the promise.
- That Little Built is the final brand name.
- That any candidate meets quality or economics gates.
- That the three-product collection will convert.
- That the products are available from an acceptable supplier.

## Decision

The brand is stronger than the current product set in structure. Storefront Zero may now be evaluated as a buying experience. Supplier outreach remains unauthorized until the founder reviews this prototype and explicitly approves one learning conversation.
