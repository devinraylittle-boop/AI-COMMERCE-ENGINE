# Rejected Offer 001 — Dosing Funnel Candidate Laboratory

**Status:** vetoed before exposure on 2026-07-14

No moderator message, public post, product recommendation, affiliate application, supplier contact, payment, inventory, or advertising was initiated.

## What was proposed

- **Mechanism:** Expertise Ladder
- **Assumed problem:** espresso grounds spilling during 58 mm puck preparation
- **Candidate:** ARO PortaFunnel, 58 mm Tall
- **Proposed test:** fit-qualified, no-payment public recommendation signal

## Why it was vetoed

ACE had not established that:

- espresso should supply the first mechanism;
- spilled grounds were a top-five espresso frustration;
- pain, urgency, actual spending, and current workarounds supported action;
- Expertise Ladder fit the highest-value problem;
- the candidate was superior to product-free workarounds or other solutions.

The proposal reversed ACE's required sequence by allowing an interesting product to select a mechanism. It was six inches ahead of reality.

## What survives

- Rejecting an easier known affiliate commission was consistent with honest optimization.
- Compatibility, no-buy criteria, regret, and nonmagnetic-basket risks remain useful **if** puck-preparation mess later earns admission.
- Public research must be transparent, permission-based, and free of sales or affiliate links.

## Reopening gate

This candidate may be reconsidered only if:

1. Coffee is selected in a future comparative cycle and `company_001/labs/coffee/PROBLEM_DISCOVERY_RESERVE.md` admits puck-preparation mess to its top-five problem set.
2. A separate experiment selects Expertise Ladder or another mechanism based on the admitted problem.
3. Solution comparison shows a physical funnel outperforms dose correction, basket correction, technique education, a dosing cup, and doing nothing for a defined segment.
4. A product comparison evaluates fit, failure, regret, price, and contribution without preferring compensated products.
