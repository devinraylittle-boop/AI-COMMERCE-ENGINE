# Validation Protocol

## Sequence

1. State one falsifiable hypothesis and its target customer.
2. Identify the cheapest compliant test that can invalidate it.
3. Define channel, offer, price, creative, audience, dates, budget ceiling, and success/failure thresholds before starting.
4. Obtain human approval for any spend or public-facing action outside this application.
5. Record funnel counts, revenue, refunds, and costs without altering raw source records.
6. Compare observed results to thresholds and unit economics.
7. Record result, lessons, and a Fail, Iterate, Pass, or Pending decision.
8. Move pipeline status only with a reason, evidence, actor, and next action.

Before approving spend, the Research Workbench must leave the `Not enough evidence` state and the recommendation brief must cite the internal records used. A nonzero generated budget is a ceiling for human review, never authorization.

## Minimum review gates

- Demand evidence comes from more than popularity alone and has provenance, time, geography, and confidence.
- Contribution margin remains positive under an explicit downside scenario.
- Break-even CAC is above the test’s observed or credibly estimated CAC.
- Supplier identity, quote, MOQ, lead time, and quality evidence are independently reviewed.
- Trademark, counterfeit, regulated-product, claims, privacy, platform, returns, and shipping risks are reviewed by a qualified human where applicable.
- A validation pass is not permission to publish, order, or scale.

## Integrity rules

Do not invent missing observations, backfill desired outcomes, mix estimates with observed data, or reuse one experiment’s evidence as though it directly validates another audience or offer. Preserve source exports outside the application when feasible and link or describe them in evidence notes.
